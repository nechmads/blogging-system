# 01 — Cover Stock · draft design notes (this prototype only)

Static prototype: `index.html` (home) and `post.html` (article), embedded
CSS/JS, local `fonts/` and `img/`. Generated by `_build/c1.py`. These tokens
describe this prototype, not an application-wide system.

## Originality gate

- **Probable generic answer refused:** a 3-up grid of uniform rounded cover
  cards under a centred masthead, each with a thumbnail, tag chips and a
  "Read more" button — a card grid wearing a costume.
- **Content-native rule:** *issue → cover → shelf.* Every post is an issue with
  a cover generated from its own type (series band: publication + issue
  number; title zone: kicker + title; illustration zone: image inset on a mat,
  or the deck in Source Serif italic when there is no image; imprint foot:
  author · date).
  One fixed grid for every cover, so the eight posts read as one series while
  the plates and heights vary. The home page is a face-out shelf: the latest
  issue faces out at full size, the seven back issues stand beside it on one
  shelf line in staggered sizes, and the catalogue below is the same list
  again as a stamped date column. The article opens on its own cover.
- **First-viewport silhouette and type roles:** a tall portrait rectangle on
  the left, a row of shorter portrait rectangles standing on one horizontal
  shelf line to its right, all on warm paper; the publication name is a small
  imprint above, never a hero masthead. Fraunces (opsz 144, wght 600) is the
  single bold role — cover/article title — and Fraunces 500 the h2s; Source
  Serif 4 italic carries decks, the publisher's note and the catalogue backs
  (the bundled Fraunces has no WONK/SOFT axis, so its italic "h" reads like a
  ligature at deck size — verified by rendering test); Instrument Sans 400/500
  does band, imprint, catalogue, nav, forms and comment metadata; Source Serif
  4 is the article body at 1.125rem / 1.6 in a 66ch column.
- **Fallbacks that would invalidate it:** rounded corners, a per-cover shadow
  or border, uniform cover heights in a tidy grid, a centred masthead hero, tag
  chips, per-item "Read more" buttons, the raw accent behind text, images as
  the cover's main event.
- **How it still serves the job:** the lead is the largest object on the page
  and is a link; the catalogue is a plain date-first list for scanning; the
  article opens on its full cover (standing on the shelf line with one
  standfirst and byline beside it), then a half-size facsimile pins beside the
  66ch reading column once the cover has scrolled away, so the accent recedes
  for the long read; sources, subjects, comments and subscribe sit in the
  column.

## Tokens

```
--ground        oklch(93.5% 0.016 82)   warm paper page ground
--stock         oklch(97.5% 0.008 85)   cover stock (paper plate, paper type on deep plates)
--shelf         oklch(85% 0.022 78)     shelf edge and footer band
--ink           oklch(22% 0.012 60)     warm near-black
--ink-2         oklch(42% 0.012 60)     muted text (7.0:1 on ground)
--rule          oklch(80% 0.014 80)     catalogue and list rules
--sans          Instrument Sans 400/500
--disp          Fraunces (variable: opsz 9–144, wght 300–900, italic)
--body          Source Serif 4 400/600
--pad           clamp(16px, 3.4vw, 48px)
--lift          160ms cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)   (disabled under reduced motion)
```

Cover grid (rows in container-query width units of the cover): band 14cqw ·
title 1fr · illustration 50cqw · foot 12cqw, aspect 2:3. Lead and article
cover on mobile: 3:4 with rows 12 / 1fr / 40 / 10. Title size steps down by
title length: ≤30 chars 14cqw, ≤40 12cqw, ≤56 10cqw, longer 9cqw (face-out
covers ×1.12), each with a rem floor so shelved covers stay legible.

Sizes: face-out cover `clamp(300px, min(27vw, 46vh), 440px)` wide; shelved
covers `19.5vw × {1, .95, 1.06, .97, 1.03, .94, 1.07}` (≈ 72% of the lead's
height, staggered), `38vw` on mobile, `15vw` on the article's end shelf; the
pinned facsimile is 250px. Illustration: the photo sits flush in the zone
(no mat), `object-fit: cover`, greyscaled and blended with
`mix-blend-mode: luminosity` so it takes the plate's hue and chroma — a
derived duotone: brick on the sample red, olive-gold on `#ffd400`, navy on
`#0a0a2a`, warm grey on the stock plate. No text sits over a photo, so no
contrast pair is introduced. Imprint foot: long date ≥330px cover,
"Mon D, YYYY" below that, author dropped below 190px (container queries), so a
date never runs under a neighbour.

Shelf edge: a script fits each shelf's width to *n* whole covers plus a 40px
spine of the next one (recomputed on resize), ending inside the page margin.
The cut cover gets `.is-partial` (IntersectionObserver, root = the row), which
hides its face and shows its **spine**: the plate colour with the series name
and issue number turned 90° (`writing-mode: vertical-rl`), no rules. Titles
are never sliced; the spine is the scroll cue, and the ‹ › buttons and arrow
keys page the shelf.

Dates: one register outside the catalogue — "Sep 2, 2026" — on cover feet,
the byline and comments; the catalogue keeps ISO dates as its card-index
contrast. Section labels step down below CATALOGUE (.74rem / 400 / ink-2;
form labels .72rem) so the lower page tapers.

Face-out covers: title ×1.25 and deck 5.8cqw (5.3cqw on the 3:4 mobile
variant, whose illustration zone is 44cqw) so the title zone and the deck
zone each fill, leaving one intentional margin.

## Accent derivations (CSS relative colour)

`--publication-accent` is set on `<html>` (default `#b4361f`). Nothing that
carries legibility uses the hex directly.

```
--accent-ink   oklch(from var(--publication-accent) clamp(0.25, l, 0.42) min(c, 0.15) h)
               deep plate fill; issue numbers; source numbers; focus ring; paper-plate band fill
--plate-deep   var(--accent-ink)                     paper (--stock) type on it
--plate-tint   oklch(from var(--publication-accent) 0.92 min(c, 0.08) h)
               ink type on it; also the comment initials tile
--accent-line  oklch(from var(--publication-accent) clamp(0.3, l, 0.5) min(c, 0.17) h)
               link underlines, catalogue "Turn" rule, RSS dot — marks, not text
```

Plates rotate by post index: deep, tint, paper (paper plate = stock ground with
an `--accent-ink` series band).

## Interactions

- Shelf: horizontal scroll with proximity snap (mandatory on mobile); ← → move
  focus between covers when a cover is focused; ‹ › buttons page the shelf;
  hover/focus lifts a cover 8px with the design's only shadow (no lift under
  `prefers-reduced-motion`).
- Catalogue: each row is the link to its issue; one "Show summaries" control
  in the catalogue header (`aria-pressed`) turns every issue over at once
  (deck + subjects). No per-row controls.
- Post: the opening and the body share one two-column grid
  (`cover-w | minmax(0, 68ch)`, centred as a pair). The opening holds the
  standfirst (first baseline on the cover's title baseline), the byline and
  the first two paragraphs, so it runs to the shelf line; the rest reads
  below. Once the opening scrolls off, a 250px facsimile pins at the right of
  the cover column on desktop (`position: sticky`, revealed by
  IntersectionObserver; 220ms fade, none under reduced motion). On narrow
  screens the running head (issue number + title) sits in flow at the body's
  start — the mobile shelf line — and then pins to the top (plain sticky).
- Forms mark the failing field `aria-invalid` and clear it on success; the
  note is `aria-live`.
- The home page's `h1` is the imprint; the article's `h1` is on its cover.
- Forms (`method="dialog"`, `novalidate`): local validation with an inline
  message, then "Demo only — nothing was sent." Nothing leaves the page.

## Remaining gaps (from the final independent assessment — for the porter)

- The desktop shelf shows three face-outs plus one spine and pages with the
  ‹ › buttons rather than standing all seven back issues on the shelf at once.
- On the article, the shelf line crosses the reading column, and the sticky
  facsimile sits at x≈324 (right of the cover column) while the opening cover
  sits at x≈185, so the cover appears to shift when it shrinks.
- The article cover's band and foot are set in the serif (the opening/post
  grid sets `font-family: var(--body)`, which the cover inherits), while the
  home covers' band and foot are Instrument Sans.
