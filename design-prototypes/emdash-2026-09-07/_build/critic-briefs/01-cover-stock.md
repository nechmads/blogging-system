CHOSEN DIRECTION — "Cover Stock": every post is an issue with a generated typographic cover; the cover is built from title, kicker and date on a fixed cover grid (series band, title zone, illustration zone, imprint foot) so it needs no photograph. The home page is a bookshop shelf: the lead issue faces out at full size on the left, the seven others stand beside it on one shelf line, staggered like real books; below the shelf a plain catalogue list with dates in a stamped column. The article opens on its own cover, which stays beside the text on desktop. References translated: Marber-grid paperback series, record-shop face-out shelf, library catalogue card — with their literal motifs (penguin orange, vinyl texture, rubber stamps) rejected.

INTENDED FEELING: a considered series of small books from a publisher with a house style — collected, warm, tactile without texture.

SIGNATURE IDEA: one issue facing out, the others standing beside it on a shelf; an article that opens on its own cover. Cover plates are colour variants derived from the publication's accent.

TYPE ROLES: Fraunces for cover titles and article headings (the only bold role), Instrument Sans for series band, metadata, list and UI, Source Serif for the article body.

MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO: a grid of rounded cover cards; a centred masthead hero above the shelf; tag chips; per-item "read more" buttons; image-led covers.
