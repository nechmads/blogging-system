PRODUCT: A blog template for Hot Metal, a multi-publication publishing platform. One template is chosen per publication and must look right for any publication that picks it, not only the sample one. Sample content is the real "Looking Ahead" publication: notes from a five-time AI founder on AI, strategy and geopolitics.

AUDIENCE: Readers of an independent expert's publication, arriving from X/LinkedIn/search, skimming the home page, then reading one 1,500–2,500 word argument.

PRIMARY TASK: Home page — make one article irresistible and the archive scannable and credible at a glance. Article page — sustained long-form reading with in-text links, a real citations block and comments.

REQUIRED CONTENT: publication name/tagline/description, nav, RSS and social links, a lead story, seven archive entries, tags, a long article with sub-headings and links, a sources block of 13 citations, three comments and a comment form, a subscribe form, footer.

CONSTRAINTS THAT SHAPE THE DESIGN: (1) Featured images are AI-generated, always square, uneven in quality and often missing — the lead story has none; a template must make a missing image look intentional. (2) The accent colour is an arbitrary per-publication hex, so the design must not depend on this particular red; any tinted element must remain legible if the hue were pale yellow or near-black. (3) Citations, comments, RSS and tags are required surfaces.

TONE: authoritative, forward-looking, human; not corporate-AI. The brief asked for something unique and eye-catching that still does the reading job.

EXCLUSIONS: interchangeable rounded-card grids; purple/blue gradients; glassmorphism; decorative blobs, glows, particles; a newspaper broadsheet; a dark monospaced dispatch log; gallery-catalogue plates; a fixed accent-coloured side panel; an annotated margin notebook.
