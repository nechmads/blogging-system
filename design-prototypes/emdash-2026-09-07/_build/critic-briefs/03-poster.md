CHOSEN DIRECTION — "Poster": the headline is the argument. The lead title fills the first screen edge to edge at poster scale, ragged, with the deck small in the negative space and the publication name running vertically up the right edge; nav is a small fixed cluster. The archive is a wall of posters: full-width bands with large titles alternating left/right alignment, separated by whitespace only — no rules, no containers, no chips. Images exist only on hover/focus (a small square following the cursor) or as a small inline square on mobile, so a missing image costs nothing. The article page is deliberately calm: a disciplined reading column with the poster scale returning only in pull quotes.

INTENDED FEELING: bold, confident, direct — a bill-poster wall, a manifesto — with a calm, disciplined reading page.

SIGNATURE IDEA: the lead claim consuming the screen; images that exist only when you reach for them.

TYPE ROLES: one sans family (Bricolage Grotesque) for all display and UI across a huge scale range, hierarchy from optical size and width rather than mixed families; Geist for the article body. Heavy weight in exactly one role per page (titles). Pure white and black; the accent appears once per screen as a derived highlight or underline.

MUST NOT COLLAPSE INTO: a centred landing-page hero; outline or gradient text; cards or chips; hairline rules on the home page; a hero image; monospaced anything; more than one bold role.
